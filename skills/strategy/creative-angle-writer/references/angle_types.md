# Angle Types Reference

**Used by:** creative-angle-writer  
**Purpose:** Definitions and usage guidelines for each angle type

---

## 1. Problem-Solution

**Lead Type:** EMOTIONAL  
**Core mechanic:** Name the pain → validate it → reveal the product as relief  
**When to use:** Customer has a documented, specific pain point that the product directly removes  
**Hook structure:** Start with the pain in customer language, not with the product  
**Polish buyer note:** Works best when the pain is social (embarrassment, being left behind) or functional (gear-blame anxiety)

**Example:** GAM-A01 (Sprzęt Mnie Wydał) — gear-blame anxiety → 280Hz removes the variable

---

## 2. Mechanism Reveal

**Lead Type:** DIRECT CLAIM  
**Core mechanic:** Lead with the spec-price ratio as the hook; the mechanism explains why the price is possible  
**When to use:** The spec-to-price ratio is genuinely surprising; buyer is spec-literate  
**Hook structure:** Numbers first — "280Hz za 859 PLN" IS the headline  
**Polish buyer note:** Fires the "stosunek jakości do ceny" identity response — buyer feels smart for finding it

**Example:** GAM-A03 (280Hz za 859 PLN) — spec-price shock → direct manufacturer model explains it

---

## 3. Risk Reversal

**Lead Type:** EMOTIONAL  
**Core mechanic:** Reveal a risk the buyer didn't know they had → show the guarantee that eliminates it  
**When to use:** There is a documented purchase anxiety unique to this category; the client has an exclusive guarantee  
**Hook structure:** Lead with the injustice (ISO standard allows 5 dead pixels), then reveal the protection  
**Polish buyer note:** Most powerful angle when the guarantee is exclusive to the official store

**Example:** GAM-A02 (Zero Martwych Pikseli) — ISO standard reveal → zero dead pixel guarantee

---

## 4. Comparison

**Lead Type:** DIRECT CLAIM  
**Core mechanic:** Same product, different protection/terms — make the difference visible and specific  
**When to use:** Buyer is at the "where to buy" decision stage; has already decided on the product  
**Hook structure:** State the comparison directly with specific numbers (3 years vs 5 years, 5 pixels vs 0)  
**Polish buyer note:** Optimal for retargeting buyers who found the product on Ceneo

**Example:** GAM-A05 (Oficjalny Sklep vs x-kom) — same monitor, exclusive official store benefits

---

## 5. Loss Aversion

**Lead Type:** EMOTIONAL or HYBRID  
**Core mechanic:** Show what the buyer risks losing by NOT buying (or by buying the wrong thing)  
**When to use:** Buyer is considering an alternative (OLED, competitor brand) with documented downsides  
**Hook structure:** Acknowledge the alternative's appeal, then reveal the risk — don't dismiss it  
**Polish buyer note:** Works when the risk is specific and documented (burn-in, no guarantee)

**Example:** GAM-A06 (Nie Potrzebujesz OLED) — OLED burn-in risk → iiyama's protection advantage

---

## 6. Identity-Based

**Lead Type:** EMOTIONAL  
**Core mechanic:** Connect the purchase to a cultural moment or community identity  
**When to use:** There is a live cultural event (IEM, gaming tournament) or strong community identity  
**Hook structure:** Lead with the cultural moment, not the product; product is the way to participate  
**Polish buyer note:** Time-sensitive — only effective during the cultural window; retire after event

**Example:** GAM-A09 (IEM Krakow) — Polish esports moment → upgrade during the culturally right time

---

## 7. Social Proof

**Lead Type:** EMOTIONAL  
**Core mechanic:** Surface the hidden recommendation consensus — make peer endorsement visible  
**When to use:** Brand awareness is low; buyer asks "is this a good brand?"; peer recommendations exist but aren't visible  
**Hook structure:** Lead with the recommendation pattern ("gamers recommend iiyama more than Samsung"), not with the product  
**Polish buyer note:** Polish buyers trust peer recommendations and independent press over brand advertising; this angle looks like peer recommendation, not advertising

**Example:** GAM-A10 (Insider's Choice) — recommendation rate data + press quotes → brand trust bridge

---

## Choosing the Right Angle

```
Is there a documented pain point?
  YES → Problem-Solution (EMOTIONAL)
  
Is the spec-price ratio surprising?
  YES → Mechanism Reveal (DIRECT CLAIM)
  
Is there a purchase risk the buyer doesn't know about?
  YES → Risk Reversal (EMOTIONAL)
  
Is the buyer at the "where to buy" stage?
  YES → Comparison (DIRECT CLAIM)
  
Is the buyer considering a risky alternative?
  YES → Loss Aversion (HYBRID)
  
Is there a live cultural moment?
  YES → Identity-Based (EMOTIONAL) — time-limited
  
Is brand awareness the primary barrier?
  YES → Social Proof (EMOTIONAL)
```
